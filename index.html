SARTech: Autonomous Search-and-Rescue Drone
Aditya Choudhary, Saketh Satti, Akshat Tewari
PennApps XXVI  (Most Technically Complex Hack)
Devpost

A search-and-rescue drone that predicts where a lost person will move instead of sweeping a grid: a Monte Carlo behavioral model uses terrain cues (e.g. following a river rather than crossing it) to focus the search, a Raspberry Pi camera runs YOLO person detection, and Gemini compares detections against the missing-person description. Built with FastAPI, Flask, React, and Google Earth/Maps.

Result: Most Technically Complex Hack, PennApps XXVI

HuginnMuninn: Autonomous Racing and Mapping for F1TENTH
Aditya Choudhary
F1TENTH on AutoDRIVE, 2026
code

An autonomous racing stack for a simulated F1TENTH car: a pure pursuit controller tracks an optimized raceline with speed-adaptive lookahead, backed by PID steering and feedforward + PI speed control, with a follow-the-gap LiDAR fallback. Also includes a LiDAR occupancy-grid mapping pipeline that builds a map from scan log-odds and extracts a smoothed 500-point track centerline from it.

Lap Time: Best: 7.1s / Average 7.2s  ·  Centerline: 29.7 m closed-loop track auto-extracted from LiDAR map

Icarus: Autonomous Drone Racing for the AI Grand Prix
Aditya Choudhary (Team Lead), Ethan Yung, Niyel Hassan, Akshat Tewari, Akshat Tewari Saketh Satti, Luniva Joshi
AI Grand Prix — Virtual Qualifier Round 1, 2026
code / competition

I led our team to advance past Virtual Qualifier Round 1 of the AI Grand Prix, an autonomous drone-racing competition founded by Anduril and the Drone Champions League. Icarus is a full perception-to-control racing stack: it detects gates from the onboard camera feed, estimates their pose with PnP, plans a racing line, and flies the course via MAVLink setpoints — developed and validated in Webots and the competition simulator.
